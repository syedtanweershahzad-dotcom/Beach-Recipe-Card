Beach Recipe

A fruity summer drink, the Beach cocktail is quick to mix together and perfect for all those summer gatherings!

Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 0 minutes
Total Time: 5 minutes
Servings: 1
Author: Syed Tanweer Shahzad
Cook Mode Prevent your screen from going dark
Course: Beverages Cuisine: American

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ ounces vodka
  • 1 ounce peach schnapps
  • 1 ½ ounces orange juice
  • 1 ½ ounces cranberry juice
  • ice
  • orange and maraschino cherries, as garnishes

Instructions

  1. Fill a cocktail mixing glass halfway with ice. Pour in the vodka, peach schnapps, orange juice, and cranberry juice. Gently stir.
  2. Fill a glass with ice and pour the cocktail over the ice. Garnish with fresh oranges and maraschino cherries.

Notes

  • You can use fresh squeezed orange juice if you want.
  • Use pulp free orange juice so there is no pulp floating at the top.
  • This can be made into a pitcher drink to serve more people.
  • Pineapple juice can be substituted for the orange juice.
